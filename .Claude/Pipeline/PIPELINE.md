# The 369 Architecture — Strategic Roadmap

## Overview

This document outlines the complete strategic roadmap for the zero-capital arbitrage pipeline codenamed **"Pipeline 369."** The architecture is designed to build proof of concept, proof of performance, and proof of soul in sequence—starting with the easiest access point (Mom & Pop shops) and scaling upward to enterprise-level partnerships, all while maintaining a 90% automated / 10% human-in-the-loop operating model.

**Core Principle:** Do not build a system without assets. Secure the assets first—one at a time—and the system builds itself from the repetition.

## Phase 1 — Mom & Pop Shops (9 Clients)

### Target Criteria

| Attribute | Requirement | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reviews Count** | < 10 | Under-the-radar, desperate for visibility, unaware of digital tools |
| **Website Exists** | Yes (any quality) | They have some digital presence to audit |
| **Average Rating** | ≥ 3.5 | Service is not the problem—marketing is |
| **Location** | Local radius (10 miles) | You can physically visit or call—warmth matters |
| **Trades** | Plumbers, roofers, electricians, landscapers, boutiques, salons, restaurants, dentists, chiropractors, cafes | Proven Mom & Pop categories with immediate need |

### Lead Generation Method
- Apify `google-maps-scraper` (free tier: ~300 results/run)
- Filters: reviews_count < 10, website != null, avg_rating >= 3.5
- Enrichment: Hunter.io / Clearbit for names/emails, manual call for pre-warm

### Value Proposition
> *"I spent 5 minutes on your site. I found one thing that's costing you calls—and I already fixed it. No charge. No pitch. If your phone rings more this week, you call me."*

### Deliverable
- **Free Audit:** "One Leak. One Fix." 1-page PDF (via Canva template)
- **Retainer:** $500 – $1,500/mo for "full outsourced digital presence" (local SEO blogs, Google My Business posts, review management)

### Transition Rule to Phase 2
When you have **9 active Mom & Pop clients** generating predictable monthly revenue, you:
1. Compile 9 case studies with real metrics (calls increased, bookings lifted)
2. Use those as social proof for Small Business outreach
3. Begin Phase 2 lead generation
