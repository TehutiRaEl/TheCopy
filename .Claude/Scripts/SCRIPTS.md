# Outreach Arsenal — Complete Scripts (Enhanced)

## Overview

This document contains every script you need to move a lead from cold contact to signed retainer. Each script is engineered using the **copywriter persona lens**:

- **Conversational Authority:** You speak like an expert sitting across the table—not a professor lecturing from a podium
- **Relentless Empathy:** You have felt their pain. You are not selling to them—you are rescuing them from their problem
- **Urgent Calm:** The world is moving, and they must move with it. Urgency without desperation
- **PVAs (Powerful Visual Adjectives):** Words that paint pictures in the mind
- **Psychological Levers:** Fear, Bandwagon, Scarcity, Transfer, Means-End Chain, Inoculation

**Golden Rule:** Never sell on the first contact. Give value. Build trust. Ask for the close on the second contact.

---

## Script 1 — Cold Call (30-45 Seconds)

**Use When:** You have the lead's phone number from scraper or directory. Call during business hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time).

**Psychological Levers:** Reciprocity (free fix), Curiosity (specific leak), Low-Risk Commitment ("send email"), Fear Factor (they're losing calls right now)

**Script:**

> *"Hey [First Name], this is [Your Name]. I'm a local market analyst—I don't sell websites, I don't sell ads. I just watch how customers move through small businesses online.*
>
> *I spent 5 minutes on your site. I found one thing that's costing you calls—and I already fixed it in a document I'm happy to email you right now. No charge. No pitch.*
>
> *If your phone rings more this week, you call me and we talk. If it doesn't, you delete the email and forget my name.*
>
> *Do I send it to the email on your site, or is there a better one?"*

**Deflection Response (If they ask "What did you find?"):**

> *"I'd rather show you than tell you—it's a visual thing. But the short version: your customers are getting confused right before they pick up the phone. I moved one line and made it obvious. Check your email in 2 minutes—you'll see it immediately."*

**Deflection Response (If they say "I'm busy"):**

> *"I hear that. That's exactly why I'm calling—I know you don't have time to figure this out yourself. I did the work for you. Two minutes of your time, no cost, no follow-up. Just a fix. Can I send it?"*

**Deflection Response (If they say "I already have someone"):**

> *"Great. Then you know what this stuff costs. I'm not asking for anything—I'm giving you something. If your current person hasn't caught this leak yet, you might want to ask them why. No charge. Just a heads-up. Can I send it?"*

## Script 2 — Voicemail (60 Seconds)

**Use When:** The lead doesn't answer and you are leaving a message.

**Psychological Levers:** No Callback Pressure (disarms skepticism), Time-Bound Action ("check email in 5 minutes"), Humanity ("not a robocall"), Scarcity ("one call, one fix")

**Script:**

> *"[First Name], this is [Your Name]—not a robocall, I promise.*
>
> *I'm a local market analyst. I was on your site today. You've got a great setup, but I spotted one thing that's leaking calls—it's tiny, but it's costing you.*
>
> *I fixed it in a 1-page PDF. I'm sending it to you right now. No strings, no sales pitch, no follow-up calls from me unless you want one.*
>
> *If it helps, great. If not, trash it. Either way, I wish you a good week.*
>
> *Check your email in 5 minutes. That's it—take care."*

**Alternative Voicemail (After Hours — More Urgent):**

> *"[First Name], [Your Name] here. I was on your site tonight and noticed something that's probably costing you calls.*
>
> *I fixed it in a one-page document. I'm not going to call you back. I'm not going to follow up. This is it—one call, one fix.*
>
> *If it works for you, you know where to find me. If not, no hard feelings.*
>
> *Check your email now. Take care."*

## Script 3 — Direct Voice Memo (60 Seconds)

**Use When:** You have the lead's mobile number from Google Maps, social media, or referral. Send via text/WhatsApp/Instagram DM.

**Psychological Levers:** Immediacy (fix while reading), Tangible Proof (see the change), Low Friction ("text me back"), Fear Factor (they're losing calls right now)

**Script:**

> *"[First Name], [Your Name] here. I'm not a bot—I'm a local market guy.*
>
> *I looked at your site for 5 minutes. You're missing calls because your phone number is buried below a giant image that takes 4 seconds to load.*
>
> *I moved it to the top-right corner in a sample I just made. I'm texting it to you right now.*
>
> *If your phone rings more this week, text me back. If not, ignore it. No hard feelings.*
>
> *Check your texts in 1 minute—I'm sending it now."*

**Alternative Voice Memo (For Text-Only Leads — More Conversational):**

> *"[First Name], this is [Your Name]. I know you're busy, so I'll be quick.*
>
> *I audited your site. You're losing calls because [specific issue]. I fixed it.*
>
> *Check your email—there's a PDF called 'One Leak. One Fix.' It takes 2 minutes to implement.*
>
> *If your phone rings more this week, you know where to find me. If not, ignore it. Either way, good luck with the business."*

## Script 4 — Day 3 Follow-Up (Text/Email)

**Use When:** 3 days have passed since the initial outreach and the lead hasn't replied.

**Psychological Levers:** FOMO ("moving on to other businesses"), Second Freebie (Google Reviews template), Binary Choice ("YES" or silence), Scarcity ("I'm moving on")

**Script:**

> *"[First Name], checking in—not to sell, just to ask: did you make that change? If yes, have you noticed any difference? If no, no pressure—it's yours to keep either way.*
>
> *I'm moving on to other businesses this week, but I wanted to leave you with one more observation I didn't include in the PDF: your Google My Business listing has [X] reviews from [Y] years ago and none since. That's costing you trust. I have a 5-minute fix for that too—no charge, just a template.*
>
> *If you want it, reply 'YES'. If not, I'll assume you're all set. Either way, best of luck."*

**If They Reply "YES":**

> *"Great. I'm sending you the Google Reviews template now. Same terms—no charge, no obligation. If you want to talk about the bigger picture after you implement it, I'm here."*
>
> *[Send Google Reviews Template PDF]*

**If They Reply "MORE" (from the PDF's CTA):**

> *"Got it. I'll send you the full breakdown of the other 4 leaks I spotted. This one goes deeper—it's a 5-page document with recommendations on your entire digital presence.*
>
> *No charge. But I will ask: after you read it, if you want me to implement everything, we can talk about a retainer. Fair?"*
>
> *[Send Full Audit PDF]*

## Script 5 — Retainer Pitch (Second Call)

**Use When:** The lead has implemented the free fix, seen results (e.g., 2-3 extra calls), and has replied "YES" or "MORE" to your follow-up.

**Psychological Levers:** Social Proof (case studies), Loss Aversion ("leaving money on the table"), Low-Ball Commitment (start with a small retainer), Means-End Chain (buy the outcome, not the service)

**Script:**

> *"[First Name], [Your Name]. You've had the free fix for about a week. I'm curious—did you see any difference in calls?"*

**[Pause for their answer. If they say "Yes, I got a few more calls"—proceed. If "No, I haven't implemented it yet"—go to the Soft Close.]**

**Close (If They Saw Results):**

> *"That's what I figured. Look, I told you I'd find the other 4 leaks I spotted. And I did. But here's the thing—I don't want to send you 4 more PDFs and leave you to figure it out yourself. You're busy, you need results, and you don't have time to learn SEO or copywriting.*
>
> *Here's what I propose: I take over your entire digital presence. Google My Business, your website copy, your reviews, your local SEO. I do it all. You don't have to think about it.*
>
> *I charge $[500-1,500]/month for this. That's what? One job? Two jobs? It pays for itself before the month ends.*
>
> *I can start this week. Should I send you a proposal?"*

**Soft Close (If They Haven't Implemented the Free Fix):**

> *"I hear you. Busy week. Totally understand.*
>
> *Here's a thought: instead of you having to find time to implement the fixes, what if I just did it for you? I manage your Google My Business, your website copy, and make sure you show up in local search. You don't have to learn anything.*
>
> *I can start with a one-month trial—$[500-1,500]/mo. If you don't see a lift in calls by day 30, you cancel. No penalty.*
>
> *Interested?"*

## Objection Handlers — Retainer Pitch

| Objection | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"I can do it myself."** | *"You absolutely can. But it took me a year of trial and error to learn what works. You can either spend a year learning, or you can pay me for a year's worth of results and spend your time on the jobs you're already booking. Which one gets you more money faster?"* |
| **"It's too expensive."** | *"I get that. What's one job worth to you? On average, $600? If I bring you 2 extra jobs a month, I've paid for myself. Everything beyond that is profit. Can you afford not to try it?"* |
| **"I need to think about it."** | *"Of course. That's fair. Let me send you a proposal with the package breakdown. I'll give you 7 days to review. After that, I'll assume you're not interested and I won't bother you again. Fair?"* |

## The 10% Human Filter Checklist — Apply Before Every Send

Before sending any script, apply these 5 filters:

| # | Question | If "No" — Fix It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Does this sound like it was written by someone who *knows* struggle? | Add a line like: "I know how hard it is to get people to pick up the phone. I've been there." |
| 2 | Does it acknowledge the client's *existing* creativity (not just their gaps)? | Add: "Your site already has the bones. We just need to move one thing." |
| 3 | Is the call-to-action *urgent* without being *desperate*? | Change "Reply now" to "Reply when you're ready—I'll be here." |
| 4 | Does it use the client's *specific* vocabulary (not generic marketing terms)? | Replace "conversion" with "calls." Replace "traffic" with "people visiting your site." |
| 5 | Would I say this to my own mother? | If it sounds like a sales pitch, rewrite it to sound like a *heads-up*. |
